Product Overview

This product is an AI-powered recruitment platform built to support large-scale, compliance-driven hiring environments. It automates three of the most resource-intensive stages of recruitment: resume screening, document verification, and interview preparation.

By combining Generative AI, OCR-based document intelligence, and structured data workflows, the platform enables recruitment teams to process very high application volumes with improved speed, accuracy, and consistency—while retaining full human oversight for governance and regulatory compliance.

The system was implemented for Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL), a national public-sector enterprise handling tens of thousands of applications annually.

My Role

I worked as the Product Manager responsible for defining and delivering the platform end to end. My role spanned problem definition, product strategy, workflow design, and milestone-based execution in close collaboration with AI and engineering teams.

I focused on translating recruitment and compliance challenges into scalable product capabilities, while ensuring the system augmented human decision-making rather than replacing it.

The Problem

HPCL receives over 80,000 applications each year, while hiring only a few hundred candidates. This extreme funnel imbalance created significant operational strain across resume screening, document verification, and interview preparation.

Resume shortlisting was largely manual and subjective, making it difficult to scale consistently across roles. Document verification involved handling certificates issued by multiple Indian states and boards, each with different formats and layouts, increasing both effort and risk.

Interview panels also lacked structured, role-specific questions tailored to a candidate’s background, resulting in inconsistent interview quality.

Product Vision

The platform was envisioned as a recruitment intelligence layer that enhances human judgment through AI—without compromising control, transparency, or trust.

The goal was to introduce objective evaluation, automated verification, and contextual interview support across the hiring workflow, while preserving manual approval mechanisms essential in regulated environments.

Core Capabilities

Resume Ranking & Interview Intelligence

Resumes and job descriptions are analyzed using NLP and semantic similarity models. Candidates are evaluated on education, experience, and alignment with role requirements, resulting in an overall relevance score.

The system also generates role- and resume-specific interview questions using Generative AI, enabling interviewers to conduct more structured and consistent evaluations.

Resume ranking & interview question generation screen

First-Stage Document Verification

Uploaded documents such as PAN cards, caste certificates, and secondary marksheets are processed using OCR. Extracted values are verified against application form data, with discrepancies clearly flagged.

Recruiters retain the ability to manually approve or override system results, ensuring accountability and compliance.

Second-Stage Document Verification

Scanned physical documents submitted later in the process are cross-verified against previously validated records. This dual-stage approach ensures data consistency and reduces last-mile compliance risk.

Impact for the Client

The platform significantly reduced manual effort in resume screening and document verification, allowing recruiters to focus on higher-value evaluation tasks.

Objective ranking and structured interview support improved consistency and decision quality, while dual-stage verification strengthened document integrity across the hiring lifecycle.

Most importantly, the system demonstrated that Generative AI can be embedded safely and effectively within large-scale public-sector recruitment workflows.

Key Learnings

Enterprise AI adoption depends more on trust, transparency, and human control than on automation alone. Designing for compliance and explainability is as critical as model performance.

Interviewers derive the most value from contextual intelligence that directly connects a candidate’s background to role expectations.
