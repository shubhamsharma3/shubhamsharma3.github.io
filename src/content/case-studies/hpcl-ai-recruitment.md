## Product Overview

This product is a Generative AI–powered recruitment platform designed to support large-scale, compliance-driven hiring environments. It automates resume screening, document verification, and interview preparation while retaining full human oversight.

The platform was implemented for **Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL)**, a national public-sector enterprise that processes tens of thousands of applications annually.


---

## My Role

I worked as the **Product Manager**, responsible for defining and delivering the platform end to end.

My responsibilities included product strategy, workflow design, milestone planning, stakeholder alignment, and execution in close collaboration with AI, engineering, and compliance teams.

---

## The Problem

HPCL receives over **80,000 applications each year** while hiring only a few hundred candidates. This extreme funnel imbalance created significant operational strain across the recruitment lifecycle.

Key challenges included:

- Manual and subjective resume screening
- State-specific and format-variant document verification
- Unstructured interviews with inconsistent evaluation quality
- High compliance and audit risk in a regulated environment

---

## Product Vision

> The platform was envisioned as a recruitment intelligence layer that enhances human judgment through AI—without compromising control, transparency, or trust.

The goal was to introduce objective evaluation, automated verification, and contextual interview support while preserving manual approvals essential in public-sector hiring.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Resume Ranking & Interview Intelligence

Candidate resumes are evaluated against job descriptions using NLP-based semantic similarity. The system generates role-based relevance scores and AI-assisted interview questions, enabling structured and consistent evaluation at scale.

This helped interview panels focus on meaningful assessment rather than manual shortlisting.

---

### First-Stage Document Verification

Uploaded documents such as PAN cards, caste certificates, and marksheets are processed using OCR and AI-based extraction.

Extracted values are cross-verified against application data, with mismatches clearly highlighted for HR review. Recruiters retain full override control to ensure accountability.

### Second-Stage Document Verification

Scanned physical documents submitted later in the recruitment process are verified against previously validated digital records.

This dual-stage verification approach significantly reduced last-mile compliance risk and ensured data consistency across stages.

---

## HR Workflow & User Interaction

The HR-facing interface allows recruiters to:

- Upload and review candidate documents
- View AI-generated verification results in real time
- Override or approve system decisions when required

All outcomes are stored in a structured database to support audits, traceability, and reporting.

## Technology Foundation

The platform leverages **Microsoft Azure services**, including:

- Azure OpenAI for resume analysis and interview intelligence
- OCR-based document intelligence for document processing
- Secure data storage to ensure governance and auditability

The architecture was designed to support scale, explainability, and regulatory compliance.

---

## Impact for the Client

The platform delivered measurable improvements across the recruitment process:

- Significant reduction in manual screening and verification effort
- Improved consistency and objectivity in candidate evaluation
- Stronger compliance through structured, auditable workflows

Most importantly, it demonstrated that **Generative AI can be embedded safely and effectively** within large-scale public-sector hiring systems.

---

## Key Learnings

Enterprise AI adoption depends as much on **trust, transparency, and human control** as on technical capability.

Designing AI systems that support explainability, override mechanisms, and audit readiness is critical—especially in regulated environments.
